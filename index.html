About

Engineering data systems that
actually perform at scale.

Over 15 years I've built data infrastructure across aerospace, healthcare, e-commerce, and government — each environment with its own scale challenges, compliance requirements, and stakeholder needs. I've migrated monolithic SQL Server environments to cloud-native Snowflake architectures, designed three-tier data lakes that delivered 30× query performance gains, and built streaming pipelines that run reliably at terabyte scale daily.

My approach bridges deep engineering fundamentals with a product mindset. I care about clean architecture, automated deployment, and systems that are maintainable long after I've moved on. Whether leading a team, architecting from scratch, or debugging a production incident at 2am, I hold the bar high on both correctness and craft.
